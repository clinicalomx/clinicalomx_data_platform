A cool neutral doing almost all the work, one jade signal for interaction, and three warm hues held in reserve for the state vocabulary. There is no green.

Twelve steps per family, and the system uses six of them: 1 and 2 are surfaces, 3 is an inset or a chip ground, 6 is a rule, 9 is a control edge or a signal, 11 is muted text, 12 is ink. Hover a swatch for its token name.

The state ladder

Seven marks built from one square frame, ordered by usability. Only the four that need a human carry a tint; present, unlinked and undeclared are ink on the page ground. Turn on greyscale above: nothing is lost, because the glyph and the word were carrying it.

presentreadable and placeable
unplaceablehalf of what you need: no position
missinga registered frame with no bytes
danglingthe pointer goes nowhere
deniedbarred: governance forbids the read
unlinkedthe slot is declared and empty
undeclaredno frame: nothing declares this role

Bare glyphs, for a select option or a diagram caption where a chip will not fit. The word still reaches a screen reader through aria-label.

Verdicts are a different shape family, so no reader compares a recipe's verdict to a role's state as though they were one measurement.

Controls

Square, one primary per surface, and the primary is ink rather than the accent because white on jade measures 3.15:1.

The checkbox is square and the radio is round, and that is the one shape exception in the system: circular means "one of these", square means "any of these".

Readouts

What the framework metric becomes. No box: two readouts separated by space and a rule read as an instrument panel rather than as two cards.
